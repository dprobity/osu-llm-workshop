Changing Oil in a Nissan Frontier Pickup

  • Overview
  • Tools

Tools & Supplies Checklist

Fluids & Filters

  • Engine oil: 5W-30 full synthetic (5.4 qt for 4.0L V6, 4.8 qt for 2.5L I4)
  • Oil filter: Nissan 15208-31U00, Wix 57047, Fram PH3614, or equivalent
  • Crush washer: 14 mm aluminum (replace every change; often included with filter)

Tools

  • 14 mm socket or box-end wrench — drain plug
  • Oil filter wrench — 65–67 mm cap-style (14-flute) or strap type
  • Drain pan — 6+ quart capacity
  • Funnel — long-neck, fits oil filler neck
  • Torque wrench — 25–29 ft-lb range
  • Jack & jack stands — or drive-on ramps
  • Wheel chocks — block rear wheels
  • Nitrile gloves & safety glasses
  • Shop towels / rags
  • Parts cleaner & brake clean — for drips

Preparation Steps

  1. Warm the engine. Run it 5–10 minutes until the temperature gauge reaches normal. Warm oil drains faster and carries more contaminants. Do not overheat — hot oil causes severe burns. ⏱ 5–10 min
  2. Park on level ground. Engage the parking brake. Put automatic in Park, manual in 1st gear. ⏱ 1 min
  3. Chock the rear wheels. Place wheel chocks behind both rear tires. ⏱ 1 min
  4. Lift the front. Jack up the front of the truck at the frame rails or factory jack points. Secure on jack stands rated for the vehicle weight. Never work under a vehicle on a jack alone. Alternatively, drive onto ramps. ⏱ 5 min
  5. Remove the skid plate (if equipped). Many 4WD Frontiers have a steel skid plate covering the oil pan. Remove the 12 mm or 14 mm bolts (typically 4–6) and set the plate aside. ⏱ 5 min
  6. Position the drain pan. Place it under the drain plug, slightly forward — oil arcs out initially. ⏱ 1 min
  7. Open the hood and remove the oil filler cap. This lets air in so oil drains freely. ⏱ 1 min

Safety Reminders

  • Hot oil burns. Wear gloves and long sleeves. Keep face away from the drain stream.
  • Secure the vehicle. Jack stands or ramps only — never just a jack.
  • Dispose of used oil properly. Most auto parts stores accept it free. Never pour down drains or on the ground.
  • Check for leaks. After refilling, run the engine and inspect the drain plug and filter.
