Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Drain the Old Oil

  1. Locate the drain plug on the bottom of the oil pan (passenger side, rear of pan on 4.0L V6). ⏱ 1 min
  2. Place the drain pan underneath, slightly forward of the plug. ⏱ 1 min
  3. Using a 14 mm socket or box-end wrench, loosen the drain plug counterclockwise. ⏱ 1 min
  4. Remove the plug by hand, letting oil flow into the pan. Watch your fingers — oil will be hot. ⏱ 1 min
  5. Let it drain completely (5–10 minutes). While waiting, move to step 2. ⏱ 5–10 min

2. Replace the Oil Filter

  1. Locate the oil filter on the driver's side of the engine (4.0L V6: front lower; 2.5L I4: side). ⏱ 1 min
  2. Position the drain pan under the filter to catch spillage. ⏱ 1 min
  3. Using the oil filter wrench, turn the filter counterclockwise to remove it. ⏱ 2 min
  4. Clean the filter mounting surface on the engine with a shop towel. Verify the old filter's rubber gasket came off with it — never double-gasket. ⏱ 1 min
  5. Lightly coat the new filter's gasket with fresh oil. ⏱ 1 min
  6. Screw the new filter on by hand until the gasket contacts the base, then tighten ¾ turn more (or per filter instructions). Do not overtighten. ⏱ 1 min

3. Reinstall the Drain Plug

  1. Clean the drain plug and threads. Remove the old crush washer. ⏱ 1 min
  2. Install the new aluminum crush washer on the plug. ⏱ 30 sec
  3. Thread the plug in by hand to avoid cross-threading. ⏱ 30 sec
  4. Torque to 25–29 ft-lb (34–39 N·m). If no torque wrench: snug + ¼ turn past contact. ⏱ 1 min

4. Reinstall Skid Plate (if removed)

Reattach the skid plate and torque bolts to ~30 ft-lb (40 N·m). ⏱ 3 min

5. Add New Oil

  1. Lower the truck from stands/ramps to level ground. ⏱ 2 min
  2. Insert a clean funnel into the oil filler neck (top of valve cover, marked with oil can icon). ⏱ 30 sec
  3. Pour in 4.5 quarts (4.0L) or 5 quarts (4.8L) initially — leave ~0.5 qt below full capacity. ⏱ 2 min
  4. Wait 2 minutes for oil to settle, then check the dipstick. ⏱ 2 min
  5. Add remaining oil in small increments until the level reads at the upper mark (not above). ⏱ 2 min
  6. Replace the oil filler cap securely. ⏱ 30 sec

6. Verify & Clean Up

  1. Start the engine. The oil pressure light should go out within 3 seconds. ⏱ 30 sec
  2. Let it idle 30 seconds, then shut off. ⏱ 30 sec
  3. Wait 2 minutes, recheck dipstick. Top off if needed. ⏱ 2 min
  4. Inspect underneath for leaks at the drain plug and filter. ⏱ 1 min
  5. Reset the maintenance reminder (if equipped): ignition ON, press trip reset until "OIL" shows, hold until it blinks and resets. ⏱ 30 sec
  6. Record the date and mileage. Dispose of used oil and filter at a recycling center. ⏱ 5 min

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting the crush washer — leads to leaks or stripped threads.
  • Overtightening the filter — makes next removal difficult; can damage the gasket.
  • Overfilling — causes foaming, reduced lubrication, seal damage.
  • Underfilling — oil starvation, especially on inclines.
  • Cross-threading the drain plug — expensive oil pan repair.
  • Not warming the engine first — leaves more old oil in the engine.
  • Working under an unsecured vehicle — life-threatening risk.
