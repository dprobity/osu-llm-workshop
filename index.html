Why Change Your Oil?

Regular oil changes are the single most important maintenance task for your Nissan Frontier. Fresh oil lubricates engine parts, carries away heat, and traps contaminants. Over time, oil breaks down and loses its protective properties, leading to increased wear, reduced fuel economy, and potentially catastrophic engine failure.

For the Nissan Frontier (2005–2021 models with the 4.0L V6 or 2.5L I4), Nissan recommends changing the oil every 5,000 miles or 6 months, whichever comes first. If you tow, haul heavy loads, or drive in dusty conditions, consider changing it every 3,000–4,000 miles.

Before You Start

  • Time: 30–45 minutes for a first-timer
  • Difficulty: Beginner-friendly
  • Cost: $35–$60 for oil and filter (vs. $80–$120 at a shop)
  • Safety: Hot oil burns. Wear gloves and eye protection. Never work under a vehicle supported only by a jack.
