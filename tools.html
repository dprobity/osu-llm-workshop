Required Tools

14 mm socket or box-end wrench 6-point socket preferred Remove/install drain plug
Oil filter wrench 65–67 mm, 14-flute cap style or strap type Remove/install oil filter
Drain pan 6+ quart capacity, wide mouth Catch old oil
Funnel Long neck, fits oil filler neck Pour new oil without spills
Torque wrench ½" drive, 20–50 ft-lb range Torque drain plug to 25–29 ft-lb
Jack 2+ ton floor jack or bottle jack Lift front of truck
Jack stands (2) 2+ ton rating each Secure truck while working underneath
Wheel chocks (2) Rubber or heavy plastic Block rear wheels

Optional but Helpful

  • Drive-on ramps — alternative to jack + stands; faster setup
  • Oil filter pliers — backup if filter wrench slips
  • Magnetic tray — holds drain plug, bolts, prevents loss
  • Headlamp — hands-free light under the truck
  • Creepers or cardboard — slide under truck comfortably
  • Digital camera/phone — photograph steps for reference

Tool Tips

  • 6-point sockets grip the drain plug flats better than 12-point, reducing rounding risk.
  • Cap-style filter wrenches (14-flute, 65–67 mm) fit the Frontier's filter better than strap wrenches in tight clearances.
  • Buy a crush washer assortment — they're cheap and you'll always have the right size.
  • Use a torque wrench. The aluminum oil pan threads strip easily; 25–29 ft-lb is tight but not excessive.
  • Jack stands are non-negotiable. A hydraulic jack can fail slowly or suddenly. Stands are mechanical and won't leak down.
